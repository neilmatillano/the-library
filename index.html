Send reviews to Goodreads

Goodreads doesn't let other apps post reviews for you, so there are two ways to get yours there.

  1. One at a time. Open a book's review and choose Copy review, then Open on Goodreads and paste it into the review box there. Come back and choose Mark as posted.
  2. All at once. Export an import file below and upload it at goodreads.com/review/import. The file has no ISBNs, so Goodreads has to match by title and author: a book can land on a different edition, be skipped, or show up twice. Try it with one or two books first, and check the review text came through.
